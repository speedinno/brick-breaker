# Launch notes

Public play link: https://speedinno.github.io/brick-breaker/

The useful conversion is **watch → tap → play → install**. A browser game removes the download/login barrier. Give people the play link, let them enjoy the first stage, and offer Add to Home Screen in Settings.

Publish your own short gameplay clips showing an actual chain reaction, a new pattern, and a stage clear. Do not claim that another creator's clip depicts this game. The code, artwork, and generated layouts here are original.

Reply selectively when the conversation invites playable versions or game recommendations. Write a relevant personal response, disclose that you built the game, and link the public game. Ask creators about collaborations. Avoid pasting the same unsolicited promotional reply under every post: X describes repetitive unsolicited replies as spam.
