## Observable features used in the rebuild

- Small, tightly packed, regular square bricks with a solid field color. There are no decorative random holes in the starting field.
- Permanent light-gray tiles outline a narrow route entering from below. The route forms letters, symbols, bends, and rooms; it is not just an empty cutout in destructible tiles.
- Yellow/red breakable caps at route exits. One ball initially travels through the channel; colored fields erode into expanding black cavities.
- Plain white balls and a plain orange paddle in the dominant skin. A small dotted launch guide is visible before the first shot.
- Falling cyan +3 and green ×3 pickups, a green width pickup, orange floor protection, and pink fire variants. Swarm growth comes from catching boosts.
- A later phase with hundreds of independent balls, rather than the old implementation's 18-ball cap and automatic fire boost.

## Explicit limits

Recording frames reveal appearance and trajectories, not the original source, exact RNG, collision constants, scoring rules, or all failure conditions. The new code is a faithful reconstruction of observable features. It does not copy creators' watermarks, reaction videos, faces, logos, or app-store identity. There are deliberate small gate additions to make every colored region clearable for stage progression. Speed control and optional automatic paddle play are convenience features, not evidence about hidden original controls.
