Delete your account and history.

Request deletion of your Brick Breaker by SpeedInno account and associated data without reinstalling the app.

Request account deletion by email

  1. Email hi@speedinno.com, preferably from the email address used for your game account.
  2. Use the subject Brick Breaker account deletion request and state that you want your account and associated game data deleted.
  3. Support will confirm ownership and explain any additional steps before processing your request. If you no longer have access to that email address, contact support for help verifying ownership. Never send a password or sign-in code.
Email an account deletion request

You can also delete your account in the app: Account → Delete my account. Deleting an account removes its unused autoplay time; contact support first if you need help with a purchase. Support can provide the status of your request and any remaining provider cleanup.

What is deleted

The live authentication account, email identity, private completion history, attempt records, player progress, and time bank (including unused purchased time) are removed. When you delete your account inside the app, the requesting app also removes that account’s device save, current field and pending assistance, purchase, ad and recovery records. A storage or connection failure can require a startup retry; keep the app installed until cleanup is confirmed. An email deletion request does not remotely erase offline device storage; clear the app’s data separately to remove those local copies. Guest saves and other accounts are preserved. Other devices may retain a local copy until their app data is cleared; the deleted account can no longer retrieve cloud history.

What remains

Minimal provider transaction identifiers, product, quantity, provider-reported monetary observations/country and financial status remain to prevent duplicate credit and handle refunds, with their game-account reference removed. These transaction tombstones do not currently have an automatic expiry. Registered purchase profiles are queued for provider cleanup, which is verified separately from deleting your game account. Email delivery/security logs require a separate retention review. A pseudonymous retired-account hash, minimal cleanup audit and random deletion receipt remain for recovery and duplicate prevention, with no automatic expiry currently configured; these records are not anonymous. Contact support for cleanup status and retention details. Shared stage definitions are not personal data and remain available to other players. If provider backups exist, their copies expire on the configured backup schedule. Separate guest saves on other devices are not linked to the account.

Guest play

If you never signed in, there is no cloud account to delete. Clear the app's data to remove its local guest save. Clear browser storage separately if you previously used the web edition.
